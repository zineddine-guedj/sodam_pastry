Small kitchen. Abundant joy.

소담 means a quiet abundance, simple, generous, and full of joy.

Sodam is a home-based pastry kitchen in Algiers, inspired by the gentle beauty of Korean cafés. Fresh fruit, delicate cream and thoughtful little details come together, one small batch at a time.

For birthdays, afternoon tea, or simply because. Something handmade, for someone you love.

Made slowly. Shared happily.
